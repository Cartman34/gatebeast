# Végétation — `TR`

Voir les [règles de l'inventaire](README.md). Emprises en cases pour les végétaux qui occupent le sol.

## Arbres

- **TR-010 pin de montagne** — 2 × 2. *A tall narrow mountain pine, dark blue-green needles in tight tiers, a bare reddish trunk, slightly leaning as if shaped by wind.*
- **TR-011 pin rabougri** — 1 × 1. *A stunted wind-bent pine clinging to rock, its crown flattened and swept to one side, roots gripping bare stone.*
- **TR-012 saule tordu** — 3 × 3. *A twisted marsh willow with a thick fissured trunk, long drooping branches trailing into the water, pale grey-green leaves.*
- **TR-013 aulne noir** — 2 × 2. *A dark-barked alder standing in shallow water, its roots exposed and knotted, small round leaves, catkins hanging.*
- **TR-014 tamaris de littoral** — 2 × 2. *A low salt-tolerant tamarisk with feathery grey-green foliage and a gnarled trunk, permanently combed inland by sea wind.*
- **TR-015 pin parasol** — 3 × 3. *A broad umbrella pine with a wide flat crown on a tall clean trunk, casting a large round shadow.*

## Arbustes et sous-bois

- **TR-030 genévrier** — 1 × 1. *A dense low juniper bush, prickly grey-green, hugging the rock.*
- **TR-031 buisson d'ajoncs** — 1 × 1. *A rounded gorse bush covered in small yellow flowers and sharp spines.*
- **TR-032 touffe de roseaux** — 1 × 1. *A tight clump of tall marsh reeds with feathered brown heads, standing in shallow water.*
- **TR-033 oyat des dunes** — 1 × 1. *A sparse tuft of pale marram grass on sand, its long blades leaning with the wind.*

## Couvertures de sol

- **TR-050 herbe rase de montagne** — *Short wiry alpine grass, yellow-green, growing in patches between bare rock.*
- **TR-051 tapis de mousse** — *A soft continuous carpet of deep green moss over damp ground and stone.*
- **TR-052 lichen de roche** — *Pale grey and orange lichen crusting bare stone in irregular blotches.*
- **TR-053 herbe salée de falaise** — *Low springy turf cropped short by wind and salt, dotted with tiny white flowers.*
- **TR-054 lentilles d'eau** — *A film of small floating green leaves covering still marsh water, broken where something has passed.*
